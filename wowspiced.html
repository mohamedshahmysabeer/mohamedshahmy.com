Wow Spiced

A local favourite for burgers and ice cream, with a loyal crowd and almost no online presence. We gave it a voice on social and a reason for new people to walk in.

Client
Wow Spiced
Role
Content and social lead
Team
IAG Media
Timeline
2025, ongoing

The challenge

Wow Spiced had regulars but no content structure, inconsistent visuals and low visibility on Google Search and Maps. New customers simply weren’t finding it.

Phase 1: awareness

  • Shot and edited 15+ short UGC-style videos: food reactions, plating and behind the scenes in the kitchen.
  • Built a look around bold colour, close-up textures and local slang that speaks to a Gen Z crowd.

Phase 2: growth

  • Set a steady monthly rhythm of 8 videos and 4 posts.
  • Rebuilt the Google Business Profile with better keywords, photos and an up-to-date menu.
  • Answered comments and ran story polls to turn followers into regulars.

Results

180%more local engagement and organic reach
15+short-form videos produced
1,000+new followers across platforms

Plus more walk-ins and repeat visits linked to social campaigns.
