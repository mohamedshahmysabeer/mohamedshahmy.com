UI/UX design

A collection of web and mobile interfaces, mostly for online shops. Each one starts with what the user is trying to do and removes whatever gets in the way.

Role
UI/UX designer
Focus
eCommerce, service websites
Tools
Figma, FigJam
Deliverables
Wireframes, UI, prototypes

How I work

  • Understand. Who is buying, on what device, and what makes them leave.
  • Structure. Low-fidelity wireframes to settle the order of things before any colour.
  • Design. A small component set (buttons, cards, product tiles) applied consistently across screens.
  • Prototype. Clickable Figma flows to test the path from homepage to checkout.

Shop homepage

A seasonal homepage for a multi-brand store, built around a Diwali sale. The hero sells the campaign, a brand strip builds trust, and categories sit one scroll down so returning shoppers can skip straight to what they came for.

Principles

Clarity over decoration. One obvious action per screen. Consistent components, so the second page feels familiar after the first.
