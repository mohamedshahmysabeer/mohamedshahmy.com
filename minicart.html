The business

Mini Cart brings international chocolates, coffee, drinks and snacks into Sri Lanka, mostly from Malaysia. It supplies supermarkets, cafés and shops, and sells to retail customers through Uber Eats, Daraz, WhatsApp and social media. It also takes pre-orders for premium watches and phones.

I’m co-founder and managing partner. I run sourcing and imports, wholesale accounts, the retail channels and the brand, and I built the software the business runs on: the ERP and an AI sales chatbot.

How it grew

  1. 2020Online shopStarted in February 2020 as an eCommerce venture.
  2. ThenWholesaleGrew into supplying supermarkets, cafés, shops and resellers.
  3. ThenRebrandA new identity to match a serious importer.
  4. ThenChannelsDaraz, WhatsApp Business and a wholesale catalog.
  5. 2026SystemsThe ERP, the AI chatbot and the Uber Eats launch.

The focus throughout has been strong cash flow, repeat customers and efficient operations, not just more sales. Sourcing is now expanding beyond Malaysia to more countries.

The challenge

The original logo said “Your one-stop online shop”. That described where Mini Cart started, not what it had become: an importer that supermarkets and cafés trust with regular supply. Orders were also arriving across several channels, and stock, landed costs and payments were tracked by hand.

The work had two halves: a brand that looks established to trade buyers, and systems that let a small team run wholesale and retail without errors.

Brand

A full rebrand with a new “MC” cart-and-globe logo in deep maroon and cream. The same look carries through to the invoices, the ERP, the store listings and social media, so every touchpoint feels like the same company.

Source globally. Supply reliably. Grow sustainably.

Delivery & marketplaces

Mini Cart sells on Uber Eats as “Mini Cart – Dehiwala” and on Daraz as “MiniCart.lk”. I set up both stores: the product lists, the aisle layout and a daily routine for orders and stock.

Wholesale catalog

An 8-page wholesale catalog for 2026, designed in the new brand. Every product has its item code, case pack, minimum order and price, and the quick order form lets a shop fill in quantities, photograph the page and send it on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp for wholesale

I set up WhatsApp Business for the wholesale side. Supermarkets, cafés and shops get new arrivals and product updates through broadcast lists.

Social media

I run Mini Cart’s content on Instagram (@minicartx.lk), Facebook and TikTok: product drops, new arrivals, deals and pre-orders for watches and phones, all in the new brand style.

Mini Cart ERP

A custom ERP that runs the whole business: stock, shipments and landed cost, sales and invoicing, payments and receivables, expenses and cash, and the partners’ monthly profit split.

  • True landed costfor every product, with freight and customs split per kg, box or item.
  • Batch stock trackingwith expiry dates and reorder alerts.
  • Sales on a phonewith branded invoices sent to customers as a WhatsApp link.
  • Money at a glancepayment reminders, a 4-week cash forecast and a price and margin checker for each channel.
  • 13 reportscovering sales, stock, receivables, expenses and the partners’ monthly profit split.
  • Built to be trusteddaily backups, two-step login and 114 automated tests on every update.

Built with Claude, TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL (Supabase) and Prisma, hosted on Vercel.

AI sales chatbot

An AI sales assistant on WhatsApp that talks with customers in a natural, human way. It shows them what’s in stock, asks what they need, takes the order, collects their name, address and contact details, and adds each order to the order sheet automatically.

Customers get an answer any time of day, and no order is typed in by hand.

Built with Claude, n8n and custom code.
