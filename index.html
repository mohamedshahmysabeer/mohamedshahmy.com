About me

I’m Mohamed Shahmy, a Sri Lankan entrepreneur and digital business builder working across eCommerce, digital marketing, technology and international trade.

I co-founded Mini Cart, which imports and distributes international FMCG brands across Sri Lanka, and IAG Media, a digital growth studio that helps businesses build their presence online through websites, marketing and eCommerce.

I build from the ground up: strong execution, real customer understanding, efficient systems and steady cash flow first, then scale with technology and automation. Alongside the businesses, I’m in the final year of a Bachelor’s in Information Technology at IIT, affiliated with the University of Westminster, UK.

What clients are saying

Questions clients ask

Something else on your mind? Ask on WhatsApp.

What can you help my business with?

Websites and online stores, brand identity, social media content and ads, and custom systems such as an ERP or a WhatsApp AI sales chatbot. You can bring me in for one piece or the whole thing.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A small website moves quickly; a full brand, an online store or a custom system takes longer. You get a clear timeline before any work starts.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a short call, once I understand what you need. You see the full quote before you commit to anything. You can book a free 30-minute call any time.

Do you work with clients outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. I work with clients in Sri Lanka and the UAE, and with international clients from my Fiverr years. Everything runs smoothly over WhatsApp, email and video calls.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of your business, what you want to achieve, and any logo, photos or content you already have. We fill in the rest together on a call.

Can I update my website myself after launch?

Yes, if you want to. I can build on a platform your team can edit, such as WordPress or Shopify, and show you how to make changes.

Can you build custom systems or AI chatbots?

Yes. I built Mini Cart’s ERP, which runs stock, landed costs, invoicing and reports, and a WhatsApp AI chatbot that takes customer orders automatically.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I can stay on for updates, content and marketing, or hand everything over to your team with what they need to run it.
