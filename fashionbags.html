FashionBags

A year running social media for a Sri Lankan handbag brand with good products and a quiet feed. The goal was simple: more people seeing the bags, more people buying them.

Client
FashionBags, Sri Lanka
Role
Social media manager, content strategist
Timeline
Jan to Dec 2023
Team
Me, with the brand owner

The challenge

FashionBags had a strong product line but posted irregularly, with visuals that didn’t match each other. Engagement was low and social wasn’t driving sales.

What I did

  • Planned a consistent content calendar mixing lifestyle shots, customer reviews and behind-the-scenes posts.
  • Styled and shot product content in a clean, warm look the feed could keep.
  • Ran targeted Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns for new arrivals and offers.
  • Tracked results monthly and moved budget to what was selling.

Results

60%more online sales within three months
12months of planned content and paid campaigns
