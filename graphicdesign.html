Where it started

Design is how I started. From 2021 to 2024 I worked on Fiverr as a freelance thumbnail designer, delivering 25+ YouTube thumbnails for international clients with a 5-star rating, and worked as a YouTube video editor over the same years. That work taught me typography, visual storytelling and how to deliver on deadline for clients I never met in person.

Approach

A thumbnail or post gets less than a second. I start from the one thing the viewer should feel or understand, then build the image around it: a face, a promise, and three or four words at most.
