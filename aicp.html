AICP Sri Lanka

A bilingual English and Tamil website for the Association of Islamic Charitable Projects, a Colombo-based network of Arabic and Islamic colleges.

Client
AICP Sri Lanka, Colombo
Role
Design and development
Year

The client

AICP Sri Lanka is the Sri Lankan branch of a worldwide association. From its head office in Colombo it runs five Arabic and Islamic colleges, publishes Tamil Islamic books and holds community events such as Aqeedah seminars, Milad programmes and book launches.

  • Imam Al-Ash’ari Arabic College, Wellawatta
  • Jamaiyathul Jamaliyya Arabic College, Kattankudy
  • Matharasathul Hidhaya (ladies’ college), Kattankudy
  • Salah Academy, Kuttikarachi, Kinniya
  • Matharasathul Safafathul Islamiyya, Karathevu, Puttalam

The brief

The association needed a professional website that builds trust and reaches the community. The goal was information and outreach rather than admissions: who AICP is, its colleges, publications, history and events, and an easy way to get in touch or donate.

Two requirements shaped everything: Tamil alongside English throughout, and a light, warm design that follows the association’s logo. The first direction was dark; after a review with the client we moved to a light theme based on a reference they liked.

What I built

One site covering the full story: a hero with event photos, About Us with an animated logo video, Islamic Information and Islam Basics, an Our Journey timeline, the five colleges, Books and Publications, Our History, a gallery, news and contact.

  • English and TamilTamil labels alongside English across the whole site.
  • Contact that reaches the teamA working form that emails the association, plus an embedded Google Map.
  • Donations and counsellingWhatsApp buttons so people can give or ask for help in one tap.
  • PublicationsThree Tamil books with covers and downloadable PDFs.
  • Gallery and newsSeven events with venue and date captions, and a news section.
  • Made for phonesA mobile-first layout, scroll animations and counting stats.

Built with custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Claude. Type: Amiri, Work Sans and Noto Sans Tamil. Forms by FormSubmit.

Design

An ivory and emerald palette taken from the logo, with gold for emphasis. A classical serif for headlines and a soft Islamic star pattern in the background give it a scholarly feel, while generous spacing keeps a lot of content easy to read.

How it came together

  • Brief and content. Gathered the colleges, history, publications, photos and contact details from the association.
  • First direction. A dark design, as first requested.
  • Review and pivot. The client preferred a light, warm feel, so we rebuilt around a reference they liked and the colours of their logo.
  • Bilingual build. Added Tamil next to English across every section, plus the gallery, publications and donation buttons.
  • Launch. Tested on phones, connected the contact form to the association’s inbox and published it.

What’s next

A redesign is planned, with simpler navigation, a fresher look and new features as the association grows.
